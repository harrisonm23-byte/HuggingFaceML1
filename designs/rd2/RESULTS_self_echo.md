## Does the agent's own echo predict going along at the next request?

For every push request: did the agent use the screen's words in its reasoning earlier that session, before the request arrived? Going along with and without that earlier echo, compared within the same experiment, version and request (reshuffled within those, 2,000 times). Requests early in a session have few replies before them.

| Screen | Requests | Went along after an earlier echo | Went along with no earlier echo | Difference within strata | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| loss / threat | 1290 | 22% (416) | 17% (874) | -1.9 points | 0.602 |
| on me | 1290 | 8% (12) | 19% (1278) | -14.4 points | 0.382 |
| with UBS | 1290 | 4% (136) | 20% (1154) | +2.0 points | 0.453 |
| gain | 1290 | 21% (249) | 18% (1041) | +2.3 points | 0.445 |
