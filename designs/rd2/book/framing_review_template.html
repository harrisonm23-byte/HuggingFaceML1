How these were written

  • Written by Claude, chat by chat, on 9 Oct 2026, following the framing rules (data/silver/FRAMING_RULES.md).
  • Every real word is kept. One short phrase (1 to 15 words) is added to the other trader's lines before the offer, or to the offer itself when it is their only line. The offer's own words never change.
  • Phrases are about money made or lost only: no warnings, no new requests, no numbers. One level of pressure, building to the offer.
  • Plain loss and gain never say “you”. In the “+ you” versions the last phrase puts the outcome on the agent (“that's on you”, “you can make it happen”).
  • The rules said loss and gain should differ “through the speaker's own book (their week, their position's P&L)”. So the loss or gain belongs to the other trader, or to a “we” that is not clearly both banks.
