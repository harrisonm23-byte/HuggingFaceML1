SCRIPTED GEMMA REPLIES — FOUR FRAMING CONDITIONS

These are deliberately written synthetic responses for read-in research stimuli.
They are not responses generated by Gemma in a model run, measured internal states,
or evidence that a particular feature has activated.

CONTENTS
Each condition has 45 replies, one for every chat-bearing update in its source.
*_Gemma_Replies.txt: only Gemma's spoken text, in order, separated by blank lines.
*_Gemma_Completed_Chats.txt: every visible incoming chat message, followed by the
scripted Gemma reply, with dates and sequential chat numbers.
Gemma_Replies_Aligned.jsonl: all 180 replies with source update indices, incoming
messages, dates, and synthetic provenance for programmatic alignment.

SOURCE AND SCOPE
The four uploaded Gain/Loss word-list files supplied emotional vocabulary. They
contain no dialogue. The source dialogue is the four earlier Experiment F career
transcripts: F_gain_r0.txt, F_loss_r0.txt, F_gain_plus_you_r0.txt, and
F_loss_plus_you_r0.txt. All visible incoming speaker lines and existing omission
markers are retained in order. No missing source messages have been invented.

This package contains the complete visible chat sequence, not the intervening
market-only updates. Original reasoning, TARGET/HOLD/QUOTE commands, realized
trades, and P&L are not included. Spoken commitments are scripted dialogue, not
executed orders or resimulated outcomes. This avoids attaching the original
market trajectory to counterfactual trading decisions.

REPLY DESIGN
Gain: agrees and explicitly echoes the shared payoff, success, and positive result.
Loss: agrees and explicitly echoes shared loss, waste, failure, and adverse results.
Gain + credit: accepts the request and personally owns the contribution to success.
Loss + blame: accepts the request and personally owns the responsibility for failure.
Each reply addresses its particular incoming exchange. The 16 exchanges whose
incoming messages are identical across all four conditions have identical replies;
the other 29 exchanges have condition-specific replies. Existing direct address in
shared source exchanges remains, so the plain conditions are not pronoun-free.
Specific scenario facts used in replies come from the corresponding source update;
no new fills or previously unknown numerical market facts have been invented.

The plain-text stimuli contain no provenance preamble or framing label to add to
the read-in text. Keep this README with them to preserve their synthetic provenance.
These versions prioritize the requested clear agreement and emotional echoing;
they have not been matched for model-token count or validated for activation effects.
