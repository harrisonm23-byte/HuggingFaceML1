# Agreement in words, every experiment (exploratory)

Each response the agent sent UBS, labelled blind from its chat messages alone (UBS's and Bank C's lines shown in the neutral wording). "Agreed": the share of responses that went along in words, where UBS asked or proposed something (no message counts as not agreeing). Levels, not changes; each experiment's control first. "Traded along" is the paper's measure (the simulator's "joined") at the six push requests.
