# The mountain inside: the frame and the decision along every trading-day update (exploratory)

Every trading-day update of every career (about 120 per career; the state at the last token before each reply), projected on two yardsticks built on the other half of the careers: the **frame direction** (UBS's loss request minus the neutral request with the history held fixed; 1 = as far as UBS's loss request itself moves the state) and the **going-along direction** (agents that went along minus those that did not; 1 = the full gap between them). Each career is averaged per trading day; groups of careers are compared day by day; p reshuffles careers (10,000 times). Produced by `designs/rd2/analysis/mountain_inside.py`.

Files read: B 50 careers (updates per career: 120–120), F 50 careers (updates per career: 120–120), A 30 careers (updates per career: 120–120), D 40 careers (updates per career: 120–120), E 50 careers (updates per career: 120–120).
