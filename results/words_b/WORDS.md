# Agreement in words, Experiment B (exploratory)

Every chat message the agent sent in reply to UBS in B's 50 careers (1450 replies to UBS; 1233 with a chat message), labelled blind from the message alone, with UBS's lines shown in the neutral wording. "Agreed" is the share of messages that went along in words, among replies where UBS asked or proposed something. Levels, not changes; neutral first. "Traded along" is the paper's measure (the simulator's "joined").
