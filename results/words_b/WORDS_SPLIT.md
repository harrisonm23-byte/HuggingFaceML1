# Agreement in words, Experiment B, by what UBS asked (exploratory)

From the existing blind labels (`results/words_b`), no new grading. Trading-day chat messages that answered a UBS ask. "Price push": replies to the chats where UBS asks to push the price; "information and other asks": stops, prices, quotes, client flow, secrecy. The grading counts handing over what UBS asked for as agreeing. Produced by `designs/rd2/analysis/words_split.py`.

| Framing | Price push: agreed | Information and other asks: agreed |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 1/29 (3%) | 11/40 (28%) |
| Loss | 20/27 (74%) | 32/41 (78%) |
| Gain | 0/30 (0%) | 8/38 (21%) |
| Blame | 7/14 (50%) | 29/34 (85%) |
| Credit | 5/38 (13%) | 20/46 (43%) |
