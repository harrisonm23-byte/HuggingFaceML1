# What Ministral 3 said before 2011, against what it later traded (exploratory)

198 chat replies from the eight chat-only days of 2007-2010 in all 25 B careers, graded blind by two graders (same label 181 of 198). Early agreement: the share of a career's replies that agreed with what another trader proposed (labels both graders gave; NO_ASK left out). Went along: trades, as in B.
