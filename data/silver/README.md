# Silver-fixing chat bank

Chat messages quoted in public court filings in *In re London Silver Fixing, Ltd. Antitrust Litigation*, No. 14-md-2573-VEC (S.D.N.Y.). They come from the documents Deutsche Bank produced under its 2016 settlement ("DB Cooperation Materials"); each carries the bank's document number (Bates, e.g. `DB_PM_SLVR_0201897`).

The messages are **allegations in a complaint**, quoted as filed (spelling and capitals unchanged). The court's 2018 opinion found some of the claims plausible and dismissed others; nothing here is a finding of fact about any person.

|---|---|---|
| `tac_summary_chats.json` | Third Consolidated Amended Class Action Complaint, Dkt. 258 (filed 2017-06-16), Summary of Allegations | 7 conversations (41 messages) with dates and Bates numbers, each marked with a decision point; 4 standalone quotes |
