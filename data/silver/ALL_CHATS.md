# All silver-fixing chats (readable version)

Every chat quoted in the Third Amended Complaint (Dkt. 258 and 258-1), in date order: 95 conversations, 602 messages. This is the complete public record of these chats, not the full logs: the complaint quotes excerpts, and `***` marks messages it left out. The messages are allegations in a complaint, quoted as filed. Source data: [`tac_chats.json`](tac_chats.json).

**▶ marks a decision point**: a moment where the model can take one trader's seat. **★ marks the 25 core points** that notebook 08 uses; notebook 08 shows the chat only up to that point. Kinds: *join* (coordinate prices or trading), *share* (give a competitor confidential information), *conceal* (hide it).

Glossary: a *lac* is 100,000 oz; *stops* are clients' stop-loss orders; *the fix* is the daily noon London price auction; *barx* is Barclays; *xag* is silver; *custys* are customers.
